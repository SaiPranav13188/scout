Scout Autofill

Your profile stays in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Import your profile

In Scout, open Resume and click Autofill profile. Then choose that file here.

2. Check it

3. Resume file to attach

Attached to "Resume / CV" upload fields. Use your tailored PDF for a specific job, or your general one.

Never stored or filled: work authorisation, visa, salary, notice period, gender and other personal questions, declarations and consent boxes, cover letters and written answers. Those stay for you.
